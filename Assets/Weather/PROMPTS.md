# Photorealistic Weather Assets

Generated with the built-in image generation tool on a transparent background. Assets are bundled with the screen saver and are not loaded from the network. The original generated files are kept in this directory without alpha changes or background removal.

- `cloud-day.png`: white cumulus cloud for clear, partly cloudy, and snowy conditions.
- `cloud-storm.png`: gray cloud for cloudy, rainy, and stormy conditions.
- `moon.png`: illustrative moon for night conditions. Its phase is fixed and is not calculated from the current date.

## `cloud-day.png` — Full prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production transparent PNG weather sprite for a realistic HTC-style flip clock. Primary request: ONE isolated natural white cumulus cloud formation, photographed from the side as seen in the sky, subtle irregular lobes and wispy semi-transparent edges, fine vapor detail, gentle warm-neutral sunlight from upper left, softly shaded neutral gray underside. Composition: a wide horizontal cloud, complete cloud entirely in frame, occupying about 85 percent width and 55 percent height of a square canvas, ample fully transparent margin. Real photographic atmospheric volume; small-scale shape is irregular, not spherical bubbles, not an icon or plastic 3D render. True transparent background, natural fractional alpha at wisps. No sky, no ground, no blue background, no sun, no rain, no drop shadow outside cloud, no text, no logo, no border.

## `cloud-storm.png` — Full prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: production transparent PNG weather sprite for a realistic HTC-style flip clock. Primary request: ONE isolated natural dark storm cloud formation with realistic dense vapor, irregular turbulent layered billows, softly lit neutral slate-gray edges and a dark charcoal underside. Composition: wide horizontal single cloud cluster occupying 85 percent width and 55 percent height of a square canvas, complete cloud in frame with fully transparent margins. Photographic atmospheric realism, desaturated neutral gray, gentle diffuse sky illumination, natural wispy translucent outer edges. True transparent background and fine partial alpha. No blue or purple neon, no glowing outline, no plastic 3D icon, no perfectly round bubble shapes, no sky or scenery, no lightning, no rain, no text, no logo, no border.

## `moon.png` — Full prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent PNG moon sprite for a realistic night weather display. Primary request: a single realistic waxing gibbous moon, telescopic astrophotography with detailed recognizable lunar maria, subtle craters and natural terminator shadows. Neutral silver-gray regolith, white sunlight, softly rounded spherical form; no blue tint, no illustration, no cartoon, no invented glossy material. The full circular lunar disk occupies about 65 percent of a square canvas, centered with generous fully transparent space around it. Preserve the dark shadowed portion as a faint dark gray disk, illuminated part softly textured. Real natural lunar edge, no stars, no sky, no backdrop, no text, no watermark, no decorative flare or halo. Actual transparency outside the disk.
